  - **The car was dented. It is now smooth.** This was the big one, and the cause was the compression step, not the model. The original 29 MB file had been shrunk by `gltf-transform optimize`, whose default settings quietly run a step called "simplify" — it throws away triangles to save space. It removed **72% of the car's triangles** (1,570,032 down to 434,982). On a rounded, organic shape nobody would notice; on a car it collapses exactly the large, gently curved panels — doors, front wing, bonnet — into creases and dents. It genuinely looked like it had been in a crash. The fix was to rebuild it from the untouched original in `source-assets/` with **no simplify at all**: every single triangle is now kept, and the shape is identical to the file the author uploaded (verified by comparing triangle counts and the bounding box, which match to five decimal places). The size is won back instead by Draco geometry compression and smaller textures, which cost nothing you can see. It also now uses **finer compression settings** than the defaults, which matters most for the smoothness of reflections across a panel. Result: **2.6 MB**, inside your 3 MB budget, up from 1.2 MB but with four times the detail.
